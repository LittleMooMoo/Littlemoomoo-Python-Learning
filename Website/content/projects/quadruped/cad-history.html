    Package the robot around modular joints

    The early body and leg assemblies established the actuator interfaces and overall arrangement. These views are CAD concepts, rather than photographs of a finished robot.

  2. Assembly review

    Make the geometry practical to assemble

    The project review identified difficult countersunk-bolt access, bulky hip structures, tight fits, and missing provisions for batteries, electronics, and wiring. Those issues became specific revision questions.

  3. August–September 2026 captures

    Open the frame and revisit the joint structure

    Later CAD captures show an exposed frame layout and revised joint structures. I used component-level work to consider material removal, bearing seats, wiring access, and serviceability.

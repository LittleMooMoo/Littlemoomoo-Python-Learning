For a simple planetary set, both gear meshes must agree on the carrier spacing. This links the tooth counts to the sun–planet and planet–ring center distances.

Compatible tooth counts
Nr = Ns + 2Np

For gears sharing the same module and standard pitch geometry, the ring tooth count Nr equals the sun tooth count Ns plus twice the planet tooth count Np.

Fixed ring · Sun input · Carrier output
G = 1 + Nr / Ns
PCDcarrier = m(Ns + Np)

G is the ideal speed-reduction ratio for this configuration. The carrier pitch-circle diameter follows from the module m and the sun–planet center distance.

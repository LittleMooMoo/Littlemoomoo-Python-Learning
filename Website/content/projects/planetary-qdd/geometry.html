For a simple planetary set, tooth counts and planet-center spacing need to be compatible with both gear meshes. Changing the carrier spacing affects the sun–planet and planet–ring interfaces together.

Compatible tooth counts
Nr = Ns + 2Np

For gears sharing the same module and standard pitch geometry, the ring tooth count Nr equals the sun tooth count Ns plus twice the planet tooth count Np.

Fixed ring · Sun input · Carrier output
G = 1 + Nr / Ns
PCDcarrier = m(Ns + Np)

G is the ideal speed-reduction ratio for this stated configuration. The carrier pitch-circle diameter follows from the module m and the sun–planet center distance. These are design relationships, not measured performance.

I parameterized the cycloidal disk and pin geometry in SolidWorks, then worked through eccentricity, shaft and bearing support, output interfaces, and the housing. Printing and assembling prototypes showed where fits needed revision.

Speed, torque, and efficiency
ωout = ωmotor / G
τout ≈ ηGτmotor
η = Pout / Pin

G is the reduction ratio, and η is the efficiency at a stated operating point. Back-driving resistance needs its own test.

I parameterized the cycloidal disk and pin geometry in SolidWorks, then worked through eccentricity, shaft and bearing support, output interfaces, and the printed housing. Repeated prototypes made fits and assembly behavior visible.

Useful output depends on the losses
ωout = ωmotor / G
τout ≈ ηGτmotor
η = Pout / Pin

G is the reduction ratio. Efficiency η must be measured at a stated operating point. Forward efficiency and back-driving behavior are related design concerns, but one does not establish the other.

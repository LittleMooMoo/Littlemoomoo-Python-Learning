I studied how torso orientation error can become a foot correction and then a joint command. My notes work through geometric posture correction, quaternion and axis-angle error, and foot motion in a rotating body frame.

A velocity expressed in a rotating frame
vfoot,world = vbody,world + R(vfoot,body + ωbody × rfoot,body)

Here, ω and r use body-frame coordinates, and R rotates the result into the world frame. The equation describes kinematics; whole-body dynamics control needs its own validation.

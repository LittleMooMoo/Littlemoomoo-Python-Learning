I studied geometric torso correction, quaternion and axis-angle error, and the motion of feet expressed in a rotating body frame. These notes connect an orientation error to a desired foot correction, then to joint motion through the leg model.

A velocity expressed in a rotating frame
vfoot,world = vbody,world + R(vfoot,body + ωbody × rfoot,body)

Here ω and r use the body frame, and R rotates body-frame vectors into the world frame. This is a kinematic relationship; it does not establish a validated whole-body dynamics controller.

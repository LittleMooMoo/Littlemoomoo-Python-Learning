My independent study covers PD system behavior, state estimation, LQR, centroidal MPC, and PPO. I want to test whether a learned correction can improve a conventional controller whose behavior I understand. I have not yet demonstrated that hybrid approach.

Current stage

The model and training pipeline are a starting point.

The short policy runs check that the pipeline works. Learned walking and trotting, reliable terrain traversal, and transfer to the real robot still need to be demonstrated. My LQR and MPC work remains study and preliminary formulation.

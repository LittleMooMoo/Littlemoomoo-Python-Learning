PROJECT STATUS

The first custom board did not work.

After fabrication, assembly, rework, and bench testing, I was unable to get the first custom FOC board operating. I continued the robot integration with off-the-shelf drivers and used the board-debugging work to plan the next revision, with closer checks of power, sensing, wiring, and firmware bring-up.

The custom board, the Recoil firmware work, and the motor-driver interface are separate parts of the project. The robot’s progress with off-the-shelf drivers is described on the quadruped page.

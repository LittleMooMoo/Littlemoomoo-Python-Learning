PROJECT STATUS

The first custom board did not reach working operation.

The board was fabricated, assembled, reworked, and tested, but the first custom FOC PCB did not operate successfully. I used off-the-shelf drivers to keep the robot integration work moving. The failure made power, sensing, wiring, and firmware bring-up checks more concrete and informed the next controller revision.

I treat the custom board, the firmware extension, and the robot’s motor-driver integration as separate engineering outcomes. Each needs its own evidence.
